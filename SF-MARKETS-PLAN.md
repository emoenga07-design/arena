# SF Markets — How each side functions, connects, and stays separate

**Product:** SF Markets only.  
**Users:** players, fans, punters, clubs, creators — people who *play*.  
**Not this document:** SF Data, SF Intelligence, SF Academy, enterprise. Those are other products. They may later buy exhaust. They do not appear as layers, tabs, or unlocks here.

**One sentence:** SF Markets is a daily multiplayer game of uncertainty on the real world. Weekends are the peak. Every day is the product.

**The new verb:** **The Call** — you put a view on the table (side + optional % + optional why + stake). Someone can fade you, challenge you, or pile on. The night writes your name.

---

## 0. Map of the sides

These are **not separate apps** and **not a casino-plus-social mashup**. They are zoom levels and systems of one game.

```
                         SF MARKETS
                              │
              ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
              │                               │
         THE FLOOR                      THE PIT (optional)
     prediction sport                 house tables
              │                         poker first
              │                         BJ / roulette later
              │                               │
              └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                              │
                    ONE PLAYER ACCOUNT
                    (ratings stay separate)
```

**The Floor** (center of gravity):

| Side | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Arena** | The public world — fat events, fat crowds |
| **Club** | Your people — house, jokes, derbies |
| **Match** | The live night around one event |
| **Player** | Your character, not a profile page |
| **Season** | Why Tuesday still counts |

**Supporting systems** (not extra products): The Call, House vs Arena markets, four status meters, weekday machine vs weekend festival, clips/stories, resolution.

**The Pit:** optional downstairs. Same login. **Separate ratings.** Never equal tiles on the home lobby.

## 1. Arena

### Function

The public square. Anyone can see and play **fat** events: big football, boxing, F1, basketball, crypto headlines, major African and global cards, awards nights.

This is the Polymarket *feeling*: crowd %, drama, volume, a leaderboard that means “the whole building.”

### What lives here

- Global discover: Live now / Tonight / This week
- **Arena markets** — honest world-facing lines (enough players to mean something)
- Arena leaderboards (event, day, season slice)
- Festival surface on weekends: many big rooms on fire

### What does *not* live here

- Club jokes, private house lines, 40-person “world prices”
- Player career UI (that’s Player)
- Ranked week paperwork (that’s Season)
- Pit tables

### How you enter

You do **not** pick “Arena” as an app. You open **what’s on**. If the event is fat, you are in the Arena *and* can still be in a Club and a Match at the same time.

### Everyday

Arena is never off: some league, some print, some night market is live on earth. It is quieter on a Nairobi Tuesday than Saturday. That is correct. Do not fake Saturday.

## 2. Club

### Function

The clubhouse. Identity first, then lines. Discord’s gift is not chat — **a room that exists when TV does not.**

Examples: Arsenal Kenya, Nairobi Gooners, Kenya Crypto Degens, F1 Kenya, a campus league.

### What lives here

- Club feed, tone, members, captains
- Club leaderboard and club season tally
- **House markets** (local weather, labeled as house)
- Club wars vs rival clubs
- Recurring club nights (Thursday fades, midweek cup, Sunday autopsy)
- Join rules: public / request / invite / official tenant

### Two club types (do not crush together)

| | Official / large | Fan / house |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Tenant, density, brand | Jokes, local, loyalty |
| Markets | Often sit on Arena events | House + stacked on same events |
| War | Can field thousands | 11v11 still a full night |

### What does *not* live here

- Claiming 12 members are “the world %”
- Professional research / intel
- Pit games unless the club *hosts a table night* as an event (still Pit rules)

### Everyday

**Clubs carry Tuesday.** If a club is only loud when the big club plays, it is a fan page, not a club. Weekday = house slate + unfinished Calls + “we need bodies for Wednesday war.”

### Weekend

Same club, bigger Match stacked on top. Derby energy. Clips factory.

---

## 3. Match

### Function

The live experience around **one event**. This is where hours go — any night something is on, not only Saturday.

You do not open “a market.” You open:

**Arsenal vs Chelsea — LIVE**

### What lives here (one event page)

- Score / state of the real thing
- A **card of markets** (winner, next score, props) — tabs on the event, not separate homes
- Chat / reactions
- Takes and **Calls** (duels inside the match)
- Optional watch-along / creator stream (live exists to change predictions in public)
- Live leaderboard (who is crushing *this* card)
- Club war ticker if two clubs are booked on this event
- Afterlife: recap, flips, autopsy, clip seeds

### Scale

| | Weekday cup | Weekend derby |
|---|---|---|
| Same grammar | Yes | Yes |
| Rooms on fire | One smaller stadium | Many stadiums |
| Stake tables | Often tighter casual/ranked | High / Elite more open |

### What does *not* live here

- The whole site’s casino lobby
- Season admin (divisions list)
- Turning every random stream into Twitch (no IRL vlogs)

### Connection rule

Match is the **intersection**: Arena supplies the fat event (when it is fat). Club supplies your shirt and war. Player supplies your card being written. Season supplies why this result moves your division.

## 4. Player

### Function

A **character** in a game, not Username + bio.

Skimmable card:

- Rank (season)
- Stake class (what you may put up)
- Table access (who you may fight)
- Domain heat (Football Elite / Crypto Average) — few numbers, loud
- Streak, last Call, named rival, club
- Record as sport stats, not a CV for employers

### Four meters — never collapse into “VIP 3”

| Meter | Meaning | Moves when |
|---|---|---|
| **Rank** | Bronze → Legend this season | Season performance |
| **Stake class** | Max position / daily exposure | Experience + history (both directions: whales can stay capped) |
| **Table access** | Casual / Ranked / Competitive / High / Elite / Private | Rank + record + sometimes invite |
| **Rep** | How the club sees you | Conduct, Calls, captaincy — not deposits |

**Deposit more ≠ Elite.** That is casino VIP. Banned as philosophy.

### Opinion vs position

- **Opinion:** I think 70%.
- **Position:** I put 1,000 on it.

Both optional to display. Most taps are thumb: side, size, send. Why is for status, challenge, clip — not homework.

### What does *not* live here

- Employer passport / Academy grades
- One mushy “good at gambling” score that mixes roulette and AFCON

### Pit

If Pit exists: **separate ratings** on the same account (Elite predictor / Gold poker).

## 5. Season

### Function

Why leaderboards don’t die. Why Tuesday matters. Long-term player identity.

- SF seasons (e.g. 4–6 weeks) so there is always a table that cares *this week*
- Divisions, promotion, relegation
- Weekly ranked slate + weekend boss fights
- Club rankings and trophies
- Awards, historical records, rivalries

You are not only trying to win tonight. You are trying to be **the football name this season**.

### What lives here

- Ranked week packet (Mon–Sun card; weekdays = map, weekend = raid)
- Division tables
- Qualification into Elite cups / championships
- Lock times, scoring: accuracy + difficulty + calibration + conviction + consistency (exact formula later; principle now)

### What does *not* live here

- One infinite all-time points pile as the only glory
- Forcing every house joke market into ranked

### Everyday vs weekend

| Weekday | Weekend |
|---|---|
| Ranked picks, club night, one live room | Arena festival, wars, streams, clips |
| “I’m doing my five” | “I’m in the building” |

Both first-class.

## 6. The Call (the primitive)

Not a sixth product. The **verb** that ties Floor sides together.

1. You issue a Call: outcome + optional % + optional why + stake.  
2. Others **fade / challenge / pile on**.  
3. Room watches two (or many) names.  
4. Resolve writes both cards.  
5. Clip if it earned one (night’s best / worst / biggest flip / war decider — not every tap).

**Prediction table** = The Call with N seats (poker grammar on a real event).  
**Club war** = The Call at tribe scale.  
**Championship** = The Call as a season.

This is what does not exist elsewhere as the main verb.

## 7. House vs Arena markets

| | Arena | House |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Fat crowd | This club / creator / table |
| What the % means | Public weather | Local weather — labeled |
| Where shown | Event + Arena surfaces | Club + Match (tagged House) |
| Ranked? | Often eligible | Only if season rules say so |

**Never blend 12 Gunners into “the market.”** If both exist on one event: show **Arena %** and **Club %** as two instruments.

## 8. The Pit (optional, separate downstairs)

### Function

House-style tables for people who want them. **Not** why the building exists.

| Game | Allowed? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Poker | First, if at all | Players, bluff, spectators, tournaments |
| Blackjack | Maybe, championship skin | Some decision, weak stories |
| Roulette / slots | Atmosphere only or skip | Almost no player/story |

### Separate from the Floor

- Separate ratings  
- Separate tables / stake rules  
- Home is **what’s live in the world**, not equal tiles: Football | Blackjack | Poker  
- A club may *host* a poker night; it does not turn the club into a casino tab

### Connects only at

- Same account  
- Same club can schedule a night  
- Never the same Rank as prediction sport

## 9. How the sides connect (one Saturday *or* one Tuesday)

```
OPEN APP
   → Home = Live now / Your clubs / Your open Calls / Ranked left this week
   → Tap an EVENT
        Arena (if fat) + Match (always) + Club shirt (if you have one)
        The Call / chat / card of markets / optional stream
        Results write → Player card + Season + Club tally
        Recap / clip (if earned) → share
```

**Zoom levels, not apps.** Fragmenting Arena vs Match vs Club as separate homes breaks the night.

### Shared objects (the only grammar)

Event · Market (Arena or House) · Position · Rationale · Call · Club · Live session · Player record · Contest (duel, slate, war, bracket, survivor)

If a feature cannot name its object, it does not ship.

### Shared constitution: resolution

Who has the gavel (SF / stated source / club house rules / void). Disputes. No sponsored truth. Dirty gavel = costume ranks.

## 10. Where they stay separate (do not merge)

| Keep apart | Why |
|---|---|
| Arena % vs House % | Honesty |
| Rank vs Stake class vs Table vs Rep | Avoid casino VIP |
| Floor rank vs Pit rating | Different sports |
| Ranked markets vs joke house pots | Season integrity |
| Match live vs random streaming | Live must change Calls |
| Markets vs Data / Intel / Academy | Different products, different doors |
| Weekend festival vs weekday grind | Fake Saturday every day dies |

**Money** amplifies. It is not the only loop. Loop is: curiosity → Call → anticipation → social heat → resolve → name → unfinished season / unfinished fade → again.

## 11. Everyday machine (the weekday side)

Not a feature list — the engine that makes Mon–Thu real.

1. **Morning:** resolved Calls, season delta, club roast, “your path last night.”  
2. **Day:** lines for tonight/tomorrow, small Calls, house questions, ranked slate remaining.  
3. **Evening:** whatever is actually live (midweek cup, NBA, local, creator table). Same Match grammar, smaller stadium.  
4. **Night:** in-play + recap + streak only counts if you **Called**, not if you opened the app.

**Unfinished business** is the daily hook (open fade, club needs a chair, ranked locks in 6 hours). Not a login chest.

Creators/captains are weekday lights. Arena cannot staff mega events 7 days.

---

## 12. Creators (inside Markets, still play)

Host a room · open house markets · stream the event · their record *is* the subscribe button · tips / subs / sponsored *nights* (never sponsored resolution).

They are captains and hosts, not research analysts.

---

## 13. What we will not do in this plan

- Merge Data, Intelligence, Academy, Enterprise into Markets  
- Build a bonus-and-odds casino competitor as the core  
- Force essays on every prediction  
- Show skinny rooms as world prices  
- Sell Elite for deposits  
- Make the home a portal of equal game tiles

## 14. Build order (architecture, not a launch promise)

This is how the organism *grows*. Not a company roadmap for other SF products.

| Ring | Stands up | You can already play |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Event, market, resolve, record | The game exists |
| 1 | Rationale, follow, Club feed, Player card | Talk + shirt |
| 2 | Match live, in-play, The Call | The night |
| 3 | Season, wars, ranked slate, four meters | The sport |
| 4 | Creators, official tenants, sponsored nights, clips | The scene |
| 5 | Honest Arena vs House at scale, search | The city |

Pit, if ever, is a wing after the Floor has a night.
